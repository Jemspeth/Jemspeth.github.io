I am currently a machine learning and computer vision research scientist in the Vision Products Group at Apple. Before joining Apple, I was a researcher at Presage Technologies, where I worked on camera-based physiological measurement with machine learning. My foundations were formed during my PhD at University of Notre Dame under the guidance of Professors [Adam Czajka](https://engineering.nd.edu/faculty/adam-cza/) and [Patrick Flynn](https://sites.nd.edu/patrick-flynn/). My research spans machine learning, computer vision, signal processing, camera-based physiology, and biometrics. I have experience working all the way from data collection of large video datasets to the model definition and training for health applications. My applied research interest is in learning human interpretable health insights from noisy sensing data. My general research interest is in self-supervised learning, continual learning, and machine learning for signal processing.

I've previously interned at Philips and [Samsung Research America (SRA)](https://sra.samsung.com/research-area/digital-health/), in both cases working on camera-based sensing for cardiovascular monitoring.
